Subsequent illumination correction applies Sauvola local adaptive binarization, which dynamically calculates pixel threshold boundaries based on local mean and standard deviation, effectively stripping away ink bleed-through from double-sided paper while preserving delicate pencil strokes and mathematical superscript symbols. 4.3 Optical Character Recognition and Handwritten Math Extraction Handwritten character recognition departs fundamentally from standard printed document OCR due to the extreme variability in individual handwriting styles, cursive ligatures, and multi-tier two-dimensional mathematical notations (such as fractions, matrices, integrals, and vector arrows). The Rubrica ingestion engine integrates multimodal transformer vision models fine-tuned on dense handwriting corpora. Rather than transcribing characters sequentially into raw unstructured plain text, the vision model converts mathematical working into standardized LaTeX markup strings. For example, a student's handwritten derivation of kinematics under constant acceleration is transcribed into structured equations: $$v^2 = u^2 + 2as \implies s = \frac{v^2 - u^2}{2a}$$ Spatial bounding boxes are preserved for every equation block, enabling the subsequent grading engine to map semantic evaluation feedback directly onto the physical coordinate locations on the scanned student paper. 4.4 Deterministic Evaluation Paradigms and Temperature Constraints In automated pedagogical assessment, non-deterministic outputs represent a catastrophic failure mode. Standard generative AI chat interfaces operate with loose temperature parameters ($T \approx 0.7$), introducing random sampling variations that produce inconsistent scores for identical student submissions. The Rubrica grading engine enforces absolute mathematical determinism by locking the generation temperature strictly to $T = 0.0$. In this regime, the language model executes pure greedy decoding, selecting the single most probable token at every step in the probability distribution. The grading prompt injects three isolated context elements: the official question statement, the complete master answer key with intermediate derivation steps, and a strict marking rubric. The rubric assigns discrete integer marks to independent derivation phases: formula citation, variable substitution, algebraic simplification, and final numerical magnitude paired with correct physical SI units.
