=== SECTION 1: VECTORWRITE COREXY SYSTEM ===
VectorWrite is a high-precision CoreXY dual-stepper CNC drafting machine. It uses two NEMA 17 stepper motors driven by A4988 driver chips set to 1/16th microstepping. The serial communication baud rate is locked at 115200. The pen lift mechanism uses a TowerPro MG90S metal-gear micro servo connected to digital pin 9. The drawing bed dimensions are exactly 297mm by 210mm (Standard A4 paper size).

=== SECTION 2: SOVEREIGN PENDANT AUDIO SPECS ===
The Sovereign Pendant memory node runs on a Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32-C3 microcontroller. Audio capture is powered by an INMP441 omnidirectional digital MEMS microphone using the I2S protocol. The I2S clock pin (SCK) is wired to GPIO 3, Word Select (WS) to GPIO 4, and Serial Data (SD) to GPIO 5. The microphone records mono 16-bit PCM audio at a sample rate of 16000 Hz. The device is powered by a 3.7V 300mAh lithium-polymer pouch battery.
